Chicken Rice Bowl

A balanced meal with chicken, rice and vegetables. It is suitable for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 150 g chicken breast
  • 100 g cooked rice
  • 50 g vegetables
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook the chicken until fully prepared.
  2. Cook the rice separately.
  3. Prepare fresh vegetables.
  4. Put all ingredients into a bowl.
  5. Add salt, pepper and olive oil.

Banana Oatmeal

A quick breakfast that provides energy before studying or exercising.

Ingredients

  • 50 g oats
  • 1 banana
  • 200 ml milk
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • A small amount of nuts

Preparation

  1. Put oats and milk into a saucepan.
  2. Cook for several minutes.
  3. Cut the banana into small pieces.
  4. Add banana and honey to the oatmeal.
  5. Serve with nuts.
